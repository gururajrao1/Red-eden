What we know, what we guess.

Every number in the lab and every rule in the game traces to literature below — labelled honestly: established (multiple lines of evidence), active research (real papers, open questions), hypothesis (our model’s bets). Links go only to pages actually retrieved; classics are cited by author/year/venue.

The agent's reading queue

No live AI lives inside these pages — the stories are written by the project's reader agent (the assistant that built this app) from the cited papers, and voted requests get written first. 📖 marks papers with a finished story.

Honest limits

No entry here claims Mars has or had life. Lab time-compression (chemistry in minutes) and single-trait genomes are conveniences, not claims. The models show what life would have to do — survival, detox, sheltering, emergence — under measured Martian chemistry.
